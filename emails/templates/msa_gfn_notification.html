Trust this email finds you well! This is Shelley from the Fiscal Monitor team. I’m writing to inform you about the change in methodology of the Gross Financing Needs calculation (in the Statistical Appendix table A24 “Structural Fiscal Indicators”).

The Gross Financing Needs is defined as overall government deficit + debt amortization (both in percent of GDP). Previously, government debt amortization was coming from the VE database. But this database has been discontinued, so we will be using the difference between GGDS and GGEI series from WEO to calculate government debt amortization going forward. The detailed steps for calculation are shown below:

Gross Financing Needs= (– GGXCNL + Government debt amortization) / NGDP_FY * 100

Government debt amortization = GGDS – GGEI


where

GGXCNL: General government net lending/borrowing, national currency

NGDP_FY: GDP in fiscal year, current prices in national currency

GGDS: General government debt service, national currency

GGEI: General government expense: Interest, national currency
